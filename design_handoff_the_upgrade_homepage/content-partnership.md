# The Upgrade · content partnership rules

**Status:** agreed 25 Sep 2026 after first contact with Hazman Loka Kirana (TikTok, Malaysian premium hotel and travel content). Offer on the table: RM8 per photo, or free if The Upgrade promotes his photography and videography services to featured hotels. No proposal sent yet.

## The one rule

Third-party posts supply the picture and the subject. They never supply the verdict. The Upgrade's judgment, maths and recommendation are always Qie's.

## What a supplier's photo can and cannot carry

| Section | Supplier photo allowed | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero imagery | Yes, credited | Visual only |
| Archive thumbnails | Yes, credited | Visual only |
| The verdict | Yes, credited | The maths is Qie's, the photo is illustration |
| Seen on TikTok slot | Yes, embedded via official TikTok embed | The post is the subject |
| The stay | **No** | First-person section. The About copy promises the stays are Qie's own. A supplier's photo of a room Qie never slept in breaks that promise |
| Your points | Rarely needed | Text-led |

## Copyright, plainly

Editing a photo does not transfer copyright. Adding text, grading, cropping: none of it. Under the Copyright Act 1987 the copyright stays with whoever took the photo or commissioned it. Editing someone else's image without permission is itself an infringement. Credit is attribution, not a licence. Once the image is on theupgrade.my, Qie is the publisher and the exposure is his.

| Source | Risk | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Photos Hazman shot himself | None | Written licence, pay per batch, credit |
| Hotel press kit images | Low, but real once the site runs affiliate or sponsorship | Email the hotel's marketing and ask for editorial use. Press kits exist for this and hotels almost always say yes. Every email is also an Axel Nova hospitality lead |
| Drone or pro shots the hotel commissioned | Medium. A photographer or stock agency may own it. Agencies run automated image matching and send demand letters | Ask the hotel who owns it before use |
| Reposts from other creators | Not allowed | Never |

Why this matters more than it does for a TikTok account: sponsors and paid-tier partners ask about image rights as standard due diligence, and a demand letter to a business with revenue is a different conversation from one to a hobby account.

## Licence terms to put in writing, one page

- Non-exclusive, perpetual licence to The Upgrade for site, newsletter and social
- Credit required: "Photo: Hazman Loka" or his preferred form
- No resale or sublicensing by The Upgrade
- He warrants he owns each image and holds property or model releases where needed
- He indemnifies The Upgrade on ownership claims
- Paid per batch on invoice. RM8 per image as offered, subject to the ownership question

## Writing from his posts

**Bad.** Rewriting his "Top 7 Oceanfront Resorts" into a blog post. Thin, treated as low-value by search, and the verdict is his, which breaks the brand's core promise.

**Good.** A recurring slot, working title **"Seen on TikTok"**. One of his posts, credited and embedded through the official TikTok embed, with The Upgrade's layer underneath: which of those resorts you can book on points, what each costs in Bonvoy or Hilton, which status actually gets the sea-view upgrade. He gets views, The Upgrade gets visuals and a subject, the judgment stays Qie's.

## Two relationships, one line between them

The barter offer, free photos in exchange for promoting his services to featured hotels, mixes editorial with vendor referral. Featured hotels are editorial subjects, not clients. The Upgrade must not recommend a vendor to a hotel it reviews.

Split it:

- **The Upgrade** licenses photos. Small money, credited, editorial untouched.
- **Axel Nova Ventures** refers photography and videography to Hazman for hospitality clients. A real referral partnership, priced or reciprocal, unrelated to the newsletter's editorial.

Also: he has no website and his portfolio is the TikTok account. That is an Axel Nova Starter or Professional portfolio-site lead. Barter is fine if priced and written down. Never an unpriced favour.

## Cross-promotion

His reach (pinned posts at 166k to 312k views) is worth more than RM8 a photo. His posts credit The Upgrade for the strategy angle, The Upgrade credits him for visuals.

## Open before any proposal

1. Are the photos all his own shoots, or mixed with hotel press kits and reposts? Ask directly: "Bro, gambar semua bro shoot sendiri ke, atau ada yang dari hotel punya press kit?"
2. Follower count and typical reach.
3. Is he open to an Axel Nova referral arrangement, separate from the newsletter?
4. Does he want a portfolio site, and on what terms?

Proposal goes through the normal gate: delivery route and confirmed numbers before anything is written.
